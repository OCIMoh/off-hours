## 1. Development — Logline & Audience Arc

### The Logline
> *In a river-and-rail city where ordinary life ends at midnight, four forgotten civic monuments temporarily reopen their heavy doors to reveal a parallel cultural world of live independent sound, spatial acoustics, and experimental cinema.*

### The Audience Arc
- **Arrival (Frame 01):** The visitor arrives expecting another commercial music festival with generic lineup posters and sponsor banners.
- **The Turn (Scene 02–03):** They discover that the city itself is the protagonist—navigating unredacted architectural dispatches from an old tram depot, a drained public bathhouse, a wholesale market dock, and a rotary printing plant.
- **Departure (Scene 05):** They hold an official Admit-One pass to a secret 48-hour civic transformation that feels unmissable, intimate, and authentic.

---

## 2. Cast the Director: *David Fincher & Wim Wenders*

### The Dramatic Problem
The festival must convey deep subterranean mystery and historical authenticity without collapsing into cheap rave neon or cold, inaccessible art-house minimalism.

### Seven Concrete Directorial Mechanisms
1. **Low-Key Tungsten Illumination (Fincher):** Deep inky blacks (`#07080A`) punctured only by warm sodium and tungsten pools (`#E5A93C`), mimicking isolated streetlamps and inspection torches in dark spaces.
2. **Archival Typeset Rigor (Fincher):** Information is treated as an unredacted municipal dispatch—precise typography, technical monospace coordinates, and raw dividing rules.
3. **The Slow Aerial Crane Push (Wenders):** The camera descends from the vast nocturnal river-and-rail sky smoothly into the physical street network.
4. **The Continuous Lateral Dolly Track (Wenders):** Sliding along the industrial railway axis through four continuous room-sized architectural broadsheets without abrupt page breaks.
5. **Architectural Reverberation:** Each space is characterized by its acoustic memory (raw concrete reverb, 7-second tile flutter echo, open-air produce floor murmur, paper-damped silence).
6. **The Lead Actor's Arc:** A warm tungsten signal beam that evolves from a solitary pulse, to an urban coordinate network, to an interior room light, to the final stamped ticket stub.
7. **The Audience's Role as Investigator:** The cursor acts as a hand-held inspection torch that cuts through dark shadows and illuminates textures as the visitor explores.

## 3. The Medium & The Grade

### The Medium
- **Mark-Making:** Typeset ink on dark paper, vector rail lines, and high-contrast documentary photography.
- **Why Not 3D?** A 3D WebGL simulator would feel synthetic and game-like, destroying the documentary reality of the historical buildings. The medium of *print-to-screen* honors the river-and-rail heritage.

### The Color Grade (`GRADE`)
- **Blacks:** `#07080A` (Deep inky charcoal wash).
- **Highlights:** `#E5A93C` (Warm sodium streetlamp & tungsten filament).
- **Secondary Tone:** `#2D4554` (Nocturnal canal water).
- **Paper Ink:** `#F5F6F8` (Chalk newsprint white).
- **Grain:** 35mm procedural film grain texture overlay.
- **Banned Hues:** Neon magenta, electric purple, cyan lasers, and rainbow gradients.

---

## 4. The Lead Actor & Asset Call

### The Lead Actor: *The Tungsten Coordinate Signal*
- **State 1:** *Dusk Solitary Pulse* — Centered on the opening broadside.
- **State 2:** *The Aerial Network* — Four glowing nodes linked across river and railway sidings.
- **State 3:** *The Chamber Beam* — Illuminating physical textures (steel trusses, drained pool tiles, loading dock concrete, rotary press gears).
- **State 4:** *The Stamped Docket* — Solidifying into the golden Admit-One ticket seal.
