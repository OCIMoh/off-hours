## Round 2 Convergent References (Testing the D + C Intersection)

### Reference 05: MAKHNO Studio by Obys Agency (Awwwards SOTD)
- **Domain:** Adjacent (Architectural experience design)
- **URL:** [awwwards.com/sites/makhno-studio](https://www.awwwards.com)
- **Primary Elements:** Vast breathing room, architectural scale, seamless structural transitions between physical spaces and environments.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Demonstrates how monumental typography and spatial environments can glide seamlessly into one another without feeling cluttered or rushed.
- **Principle Demonstrated:** *The Seamless Spatial Portal.* Moving into a room or venue feels like entering an architectural environment.

### Reference 06: Locomotive Fluid Architecture & Dynamic Viewport Morphs
- **Domain:** Adjacent (Fluid creative development & spatial transitions)
- **URL:** [locomotive.ca](https://locomotive.ca)
- **Primary Elements:** Momentum-based spatial flow, smooth page-to-page state morphs, large responsive type that scales with viewport, warm muted lighting against dark surfaces.
- **Why It's Interesting:** Shows how a website feels deeply animated and alive without resorting to gimmicky party tricks or rave tropes.
- **Principle Demonstrated:** *Effortless Cinematic Flow.* The interface behaves like a continuous nocturnal journey where the ticket CTA stays elegantly anchored.
