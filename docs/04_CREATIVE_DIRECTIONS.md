## 1. Creative Synthesis (Phase P4)

### The Core Problem & Creative Hypothesis
How does an independent culture festival with no headliner celebrities, no corporate sponsors, and no conventional music festival stage convince people to buy tickets?

**The Creative Hypothesis:**
> *By making the city itself the protagonist.* OFF HOURS is not another music event in a dark room; it is an exclusive key to the city's second life. The digital experience must not behave like a standard promotional landing page. It should feel like an atmospheric instrument: vast, architectural, deeply tactile, and kinesthetically alive through spatial zoom transitions that transport visitors directly from the city's nocturnal map into the four cavernous venues.

### Tension Resolution
- **Atmospheric Mystery vs. Fast Conversion:** While the visual world is shrouded in deep shadows and tungsten light, ticket purchasing is never buried or obscured. The ticket CTA is treated as a physical artifact (an Entry Pass / Transit Docket) permanently accessible in a single interaction.
- **Monumental Scale vs. Zero Clutter:** We reject dense editorial zine grids and card clutter. Every venue is afforded generous architectural negative space, allowing bold typography, tactile textures (concrete, tile, newsprint, steel), and documentary photography to resonate.

## 3. The Three Distinct Concepts (Phase P5)

### Concept 1: "THE NOCTURNAL ATLAS" (Recommended)
- **Central Idea:** A living, interactive nocturnal survey of the city along the river and railway lines.
- **Cross-Domain Metaphor:** The city surveyor's dark map & architectural section cut.
- **Forced Constraint:** **No vertical page breaks.** The entire site is an interactive 2.5D spatial canvas.
- **Visitor Experience:** Visitors arrive at an expansive dark schematic map of the river-and-rail city with 4 pulsating coordinate nodes. Selecting a venue smoothly pushes the camera forward through atmospheric darkness (*spatial zoom*): the city grid dissolves, and the cavernous architectural layout of that venue expands into full view, detailing its acoustics, documentary photography, and artists.
- **Visual Language:** Charcoal and tarnished steel canvas, warm tungsten coordinate pins, technical vector lines, architectural typography.
- **Primary Action:** "ENTRY PASS / 48-HR ACCESS" permanently anchored in the bottom telemetry bar. 1-click modal purchase.
- **Brand-Swap Test:** Passes. The entire structure is inseparable from the 4 specific civic venues along the river and railway.

### Concept 2: "THE CITY DISPATCH"
- **Central Idea:** Inspired by Venue 04 (The Printing Works) and the city's working records. The festival is presented as an unredacted night dispatch.
- **Cross-Domain Metaphor:** The overnight printing press run / municipal night dispatch ledger.
- **Forced Constraint:** **Zero thumbnail cards anywhere.** Every artist, performance, and schedule item is typographically integrated into monumental broadsheet spreads.
- **Visitor Experience:** A monumental, tactile editorial spread. Navigating feels like inspecting physical printed sheets fresh off the rotary press. Clicking into a venue triggers a spatial depth-pull that expands the printing press corridor into a dedicated full-bleed venue record.
- **Visual Language:** Ink on heavy dark paper, warm off-white, raw ruled dividing lines, monumental grotesque type paired with precise monospace coordinates.
- **Primary Action:** An embossed "ADMIT ONE / DISPATCH ACCESS" ticket stub integrated into the sticky masthead.
- **Brand-Swap Test:** Passes. Deeply rooted in newspaper print archives and post-industrial municipal communication.

### Concept 3: "OFF-PEAK CHRONOMETER"
- **Central Idea:** Anchored in Venue 01 (The Old Tram Depot) and the city's transit rhythms. The site is structured around the temporal transformation of the city between 20:00 and 06:00.
- **Cross-Domain Metaphor:** The railway signal box / night-shift master clock.
- **Forced Constraint:** **Content reveals strictly by time/hour rather than scroll position.**
- **Visitor Experience:** An interactive time scrubber at the bottom allows attendees to scrub through the night hours. As the clock moves past midnight, the city’s daytime infrastructure falls asleep and the 4 venues light up. Clicking any hour or venue zooms in to reveal what is happening at that exact moment.
- **Visual Language:** Transit telemetry, illuminated signal markers, warm tungsten illumination against concrete grey.
- **Primary Action:** "BOARDING PASS / WEEKEND ACCESS" button fixed to the chronometer controls.
- **Brand-Swap Test:** Passes. Built specifically around after-midnight operating hours and transit infrastructure.
